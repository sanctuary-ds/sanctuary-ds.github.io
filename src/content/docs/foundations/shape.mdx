Stone is cut, so radii are small. Roundness is spent on two signature shapes, the pointed arches, and on nothing else. See every shape in the [playground](/playground#foundations).

## Radius

<TokenTable groups={['radius']} />

`radius-full` makes a pill or a circle; everything else stays close to square.

## Arches

Two pointed arches are generated as percentage polygons, for `clip-path`. They exist only in the stylesheet, because the DTCG format has no clip paths.

- `--san-shape-lancet`: an arch on a rectangular body, for media, cards and window previews.
- `--san-shape-ogive`: the arch alone, for avatars and badges.

```css
.cover {
  aspect-ratio: 3 / 4;
  clip-path: var(--san-shape-lancet);
  background: var(--san-window);
}
```

## The quarry

The diamond, a square turned 45°, is the small-scale motif: bullets, status dots, the accent picker. It is the quarry, the plain diamond pane of ordinary glazing. It needs no token, only `rotate: 45deg`.

## Lead

Borders are lead: dark, definite lines between panes, structure rather than decoration. `came` is the line between two panes; the colors are `lead-soft`, `lead`, `lead-strong` and `lead-came`, described on the [color page](/docs/foundations/color#stone-ink-and-lead).
