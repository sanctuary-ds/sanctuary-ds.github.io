Material Design models elevation as distance from a light above. Sanctuary models it as illumination: in a dark room, what is closer to you is better lit. Use Lumen for hierarchy and Cast for state; a surface should rarely need both at full strength. See both in the [playground](/playground#light).

## Lumen

Each level pairs a lighter stone (`surface-raised-n`) with a deeper umbra (`elevation-n`). The stone stops at level 4, where Lauds already reaches white, so level 5, for modals and the most prominent floating surfaces, pairs `surface-raised-4` with the deepest umbra. In Lauds the shadow does most of the work.

```css
.card {
  background: var(--san-surface-raised-2);
  box-shadow: var(--san-elevation-2);
}
```

The shadows are drawn in `umbra-key` and `umbra-ambient`, which each flavor sets; see the [token reference](/docs/tokens#stone).

## Cast

Cast is the colored glow that glass throws on its surroundings: `--san-cast-sm`, `--san-cast-md` and `--san-cast-lg`. It signals energy: hover, selection, an active window. Cast follows the accent, so it exists only in the stylesheet, computed from it.

## Halo

`--san-halo` is the focus ring: a band of stone and then a ring of the accent, which clears the 3:1 contrast that WCAG asks of focus indicators.

```css
.button:focus-visible {
  outline: none;
  box-shadow: var(--san-halo);
}
```

## State layers

Hover, focus and pressed are layers of the content color laid over a control. The stylesheet computes `--san-state-hover`, `--san-state-focus` and `--san-state-pressed` from `currentColor`, so they follow whatever color the content has.
